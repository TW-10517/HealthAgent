What Helix does

Every result is explained in plain language — what the number is, and whether it means your PC is healthy.

Storage

Disk usage, temp & cache breakdown, large/old files, duplicates. One-click Quick Temp Clean with exact reclaim totals before you confirm.

Performance

Live CPU, RAM, disk, battery, temperature. New CPU Core Performance card detects single-thread bottlenecks, thermal throttling, and driver churn.

Startup & Boost

See every app that launches with Windows. Disable/re-enable with Undo. The Boost panel lists the top RAM-eating processes so you can close them intentionally.

Privacy radar

Per-browser cache sizes, currently established network connections, files with sensitive-looking names. Read-only. Nothing is sent anywhere, ever.

Diagnostics

Rule-based checks for pending reboots, failing services, event-log errors. Each finding comes with a safe, reversible fix suggestion — not an automatic action.

Scheduled maintenance

Daily, weekly or monthly: pick quick scan + temp cleanup + snapshot. Runs quietly in the background. Settings profiles let you switch work ↔ personal setups.

Why "honest" matters

The PC-cleaner industry is full of scams. Helix isn't one of them.

Most "PC optimizers" are the problem they claim to solve — they pretend to find thousands of issues, ask for a license to fix them, and quietly phone home with your data. Helix inverts every one of those behaviours.

  • 100% local. No account, no login, no cloud sync. Scans run on your CPU; results live in a single SQLite file on your disk.
  • Zero telemetry. No analytics, no crash reporting to third parties, no "anonymous usage" uploads.
  • No scareware. Numbers are honest. If nothing's wrong, the Insights page is empty. Health score includes a why.
  • Protected apps shield. Three-layer: critical floor (always on) + IT defaults (toggleable) + your extras. Blocks accidental close/clean of important apps.
  • Everything reversible. Startup disables → undo. File organise → undo. Deletes confirm with the exact byte count first.
  • One file, no installer. Portable `.exe` — delete it to uninstall, no leftovers in the registry.
